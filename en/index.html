2. Stays on your phone — we never see it

These are stored in your phone's own storage and are never sent to our servers:

  • Every product photo you take, and diary photos
  • Expiry reminders
  • App settings

📌 There is exactly one way a photo leaves your phone, and you trigger it. If you tap share on a product card, the app composes that item's photo into a single image and hands it to your system's share sheet, so you choose where it goes. That image never passes through our servers — but once you send it, it lives with whichever app you chose, which is outside our control.

A consequence you should know: because photos are never backed up anywhere, deleting the app or changing phones loses them permanently — even if you linked a Google account. We chose it this way because photos taken inside your home should not sit on anyone's server.

Files you export yourself

The app has "Save to file" and "Read from file" buttons for moving your data to a new phone yourself.

  • The file is written to a folder you choose, on your own device. It is not sent anywhere and we have no way to see it.
  • Once the file leaves the app it is an ordinary file of yours — if you pass it to someone else, the data inside goes with it.
  • Reading a file back never creates duplicates; items you already have are skipped.

If you disappear for a long time

If there is no activity from your account for 24 consecutive months, we delete everything held on the server, including the anonymous account the system generated.

  • Nothing on your phone is deleted — only the server copy goes. If you're still on the same device, your collection is exactly as you left it.
  • What you lose is the ability to move to a new phone — after that point there is nothing left to restore from.
  • We cannot warn you in advance, because we have no email address for users who never linked an account, and this app has no way to push a message to your device — so we're telling you here instead.
  • Opening the app once counts as activity, and the counter restarts immediately.

4. When you scan a barcode and let AI read the label

When you scan a barcode the system doesn't recognise yet, we send only the label photo you just took to an AI provider to read the text off it — the barcode number itself is not sent. It stays on our side, used only as the key for storing whatever comes back.

  • The photo is sent to read text and nothing else, and is not stored by us.
  • Provider used: Google (Gemini).
  • We use this service on a paid plan, which under Google's terms means Google does not use your photos or the results to improve its own services — logs are kept briefly only to detect misuse.
  • What we keep afterwards is the product name, brand and category linked to that barcode, in a shared catalogue, so the next person who scans the same product doesn't have to repeat the work.
  • Catalogue rows other people see carry no identifier of yours — someone scanning the same product later sees only the product name and brand, not who entered it.
  • We do, however, keep a separate record of which barcodes your anonymous user id has submitted. That record is not visible to other users; it exists so we can trace deliberately false entries, which would damage the catalogue everyone shares.
  • If the barcode can't be scanned, no photo is sent anywhere. The app reads the text on your device instead.

4.1 When you report that product data is wrong

Each product page has a "Report wrong data" button for telling us that the name, brand, category, FDA number, price or photo doesn't match the real product.

  • What reaches the server: which field you flagged · any text you typed yourself (optional, up to 1,000 characters) · and a snapshot of what you were looking at (product name, brand, category, FDA number), so we can see what you saw when you reported it.
  • Tied to your anonymous user id, to stop repeat spamming (10 reports a day) and so you can look back at your own reports.
  • Other users cannot see your reports — only you and the administrator can.
  • Kept for 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • ⚠️ Please don't type personal details into the text box. It is there to describe what is wrong with the product data. We don't want your name, phone number or address, and none of that should end up in there.

5. Usage statistics — only if you say yes

We record usage statistics only if you switch them on, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings.

  • What is recorded: the event type (e.g. "one item used up"), the product category, and the time.
  • Never recorded: full product names, prices you entered, private notes, or photo file paths.
  • Kept for 12 months, then deleted automatically.

Used for two things: working out an average — "how many days does an item in this category last for people in general" — so we can recommend things that suit you, and seeing which screens are hard enough to use that people give up part-way, so we can improve the app.

6. Shopping links (affiliate)

This app carries no advertising. Its income comes from shopping links — if you tap one and buy something, we may receive a share from the store, at no extra cost to you whatsoever.

  • Cards that are such a link always carry a "Recommended" label.
  • When you tap one, we record which link and from which screen, so it can be reconciled with the store — that record is deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • We send the store no personal information about you — not your name, not your email, not what's in your collection.
  • ⚠️ Once you tap through, you are under the store's policy, not ours. The destination store today is Shopee (via an affiliate network), which will see that someone arrived from this app, by the store's own tracking methods — that is not information we hand over.

Crash reports — what is actually sent

When the app crashes we receive a technical report of which line of code failed, plus the device model and app version — everything else is switched off at the source:

  • ❌ No screenshots at the moment of the crash
  • ❌ No product names, prices, or your notes
  • ❌ None of the app's own log messages
  • ✅ You can switch this off at any time: Settings → How your data is used → "Send a report when the app has a problem". We send these reports on the basis of legitimate interest (keeping the app you installed from staying broken), not consent, which is why it starts switched on — but PDPA section 32 gives you the right to object, and that switch is how you exercise it. Switching it off takes effect immediately, with no need to restart the app.
  • ⚠️ Your IP address: sending anything over the internet lets the far end see your IP; that is unavoidable technically — we configure it so the IP is not stored in the report.
  • ⚠️ An installation identifier: the reporting system creates one random id per app installation, so we can tell whether several errors came from the same device. It is not tied to your name or email, and changes if you delete and reinstall the app.

Synced data is stored in Singapore — needed for the cross-device backup you chose to switch on · crash reports are stored in Germany — we rely on legitimate interest to keep the app you installed working, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → How your data is used · both are outside Thailand.
